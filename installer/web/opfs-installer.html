Welcome to Awesome-OS

A first-class guided installer right in your browser — no `.exe`, no `.iso` burn required. Pick your workstation, choose any local folder or browser OPFS, and materialize a SHA-512 verified `v11` CAS root.

Direct-Drop Architecture (Zero Installer Bloat) Everything is stored content-addressed under cas/v11/files/<aa>/<sha512> with zero closed DLLs, zero telemetry, and instant A/B rollback.

Choose Your Desktop & Edition

Select a curated Awesome-OS workstation profile or minimal headless baseline. You can switch profiles atomically anytime via holo-host-manager.

Select Apps & Runtimes

All selected apps share a single deduplicated v11 CAS layer (V8 16+, OpenSSL 4.1, Zstd 1.6, HarfBuzz 15.2).

Choose Install Location

Pick any local folder on your drive (e.g. create AwesomeOS in D:\ or Documents) or stage offline inside browser OPFS.
